## Emission Schedule

### Mining Rewards (PoW)
- Initial reward: 10 NST per block
- Halving: Every 4 years
- Target: ~1 block every 3 seconds

### Staking Rewards (PoS)
- Validator reward: 2 NST per validated block
- Based on stake weight

### Inflation
- Maximum annual inflation: 5%
