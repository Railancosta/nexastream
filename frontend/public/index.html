❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay with crypto?
Simply connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.), select your preferred cryptocurrency, and confirm the transaction. Your license activates automatically once the blockchain confirms payment.
What cryptocurrencies are accepted?
We accept NEXA, ETH, USDT, USDC, and BNB. Payment is processed on NexaChain Mainnet for maximum security and speed.
Can I upgrade my plan?
Yes! You can upgrade anytime. The smart contract will calculate the prorated difference and you'll only pay the difference.
What is the NFT License Certificate?
Lifetime plan members receive a unique NFT that serves as their license certificate. This NFT can be traded and proves ownership of your lifetime license on the blockchain.
Is there a refund policy?
Due to the decentralized nature of blockchain payments, all transactions are final. However, you can cancel your subscription at any time and won't be charged again.
